# PulseNest Therm T1 (TH-01) - Product FAQ

## What is the TH-01?
The PulseNest Therm T1 is an infrared thermometer that works in two modes: forehead and ear. It costs ₹1,499 and comes with a 1 year warranty.

## How accurate is it?
Accuracy is ±0.2 °C in normal use. Wait a few minutes after coming in from outside, and keep the sensor clean, for best results.

## What battery does it use?
One CR2032 coin cell battery.

## Do I need probe covers?
Yes, for ear mode. Probe covers are single-use. Replace the cover for every person and every measurement session. Do not wash and reuse them.

## Can I return an opened thermometer?
No. For hygiene reasons an opened TH-01 cannot be returned unless it is defective. If it is defective we replace it under warranty. An unopened TH-01 can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. This is different from the BP-200, OX-02 and SC-03, which can be returned opened within 15 days with a 10% restocking fee.

## Does it tell me if I have a fever?
No. Support cannot interpret a temperature or advise on treatment. If a reading is 39.5 °C or higher, seek medical care now or call 112.
