# PulseNest Oxi O2 (OX-02) - Product FAQ

## What is the OX-02?
The PulseNest Oxi O2 is a fingertip pulse oximeter. It measures blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and pulse rate. It costs ₹1,999 and has a 1 year warranty.

## What range and accuracy does it have?
The SpO2 range is 70-100%. Accuracy is ±2% in the 80-100% range. Below 80% the reading is less accurate, which is a limit of fingertip oximeters in general.

## What batteries does it use?
It uses 2 × AAA batteries.

## What gives poor or unstable readings?
Cold fingers, nail polish (especially dark colours) and moving your hand all cause poor readings. See the oximeter troubleshooting article for how to get a steady reading.

## Can I return it if I opened it?
An opened OX-02 that is not defective can be returned within 15 days of delivery with a 10% restocking fee. An unopened one can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. See the returns policy.

## Can the OX-02 tell me if my oxygen level is fine?
No. PulseNest support cannot say whether a reading is normal or abnormal. If you have a very low reading or you feel breathless, seek medical care immediately or call 112. See the "Not medical advice and safety thresholds" article.
