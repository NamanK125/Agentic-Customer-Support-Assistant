# PulseNest Care+ Subscription

## What is Care+?
Care+ is the PulseNest app subscription at ₹199 per month. It includes automatic sync of readings from your PulseNest devices, PDF reports you can share with your doctor, and up to 4 family profiles.

## Which phones are supported?
Android 9 or newer, and iOS 15 or newer. Devices connect to the app over Bluetooth.

## How do I cancel?
You can cancel at any time from the app. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing month, so you keep Care+ until then.

## Can I get a refund on Care+?
No. The Care+ subscription is not refundable, and there are no pro-rated refunds when you cancel mid-month. This is different from devices, which can be returned under the returns policy.

## Do I need Care+ to use my device?
The devices take measurements on their own and show them on the display. Care+ adds sync, reports and family profiles.

## How many people can share one subscription?
One Care+ subscription supports up to 4 family profiles.

## Does Care+ interpret my readings?
No. Care+ stores and displays readings and generates reports. It does not diagnose or say whether a reading is normal. Discuss your readings with a doctor.
