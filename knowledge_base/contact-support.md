# Contacting Support and Support Hours

## Human support hours
Human agents are available from 9:00 to 21:00 IST, all days of the week, including weekends.

## Outside support hours
If you ask for a human outside 9:00-21:00 IST, your request is queued and answered the next morning. You do not need to ask again.

## How to reach us
Use the chat in the PulseNest app, or email support@pulsenest.example. Include your order id (it looks like PN-10xxx), the device model, and a short description. For a faulty BP-200, include the error code shown.

## What the assistant can and cannot do
The assistant answers product, policy and order-status questions and checks return eligibility. It cannot issue refunds, waive fees or approve exceptions. Only a human agent can approve exceptions, such as a return outside the window.

## When to ask for a human
- You want an exception to a policy.
- Your refund is delayed beyond 7 business days after inspection.
- Your device is faulty and you want a replacement.
- You are not satisfied with the assistant's answer.

## Emergencies
For a medical emergency do not contact support. Call 112.
