# Warranty and Recalibration

## Warranty periods
| Device | Warranty |
|---|---|
| BP-200 blood pressure monitor | 2 years |
| OX-02 pulse oximeter | 1 year |
| TH-01 thermometer | 1 year |
| SC-03 scale | 1 year |

The warranty covers manufacturing defects. Care+ is a subscription and has no warranty.

## What happens if my device is defective?
We replace it under warranty. A refund is given only if a replacement is out of stock. This applies to any device, including an opened TH-01.

## What is not covered?
Damage from drops, water, and misuse is not covered. Worn-out batteries are not a defect.

## BP-200 recalibration
We recommend recalibrating the BP-200 every 2 years to keep it within ±3 mmHg accuracy. Recalibration is free while the device is within its 2 year warranty. After that, contact support for a quote.

## Making a claim
Contact support with your order id and a short description of the fault. A photo or a note of the error code (for the BP-200, E1-E5) speeds it up. See "Contacting support" for hours.
