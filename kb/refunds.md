# Refunds - Timing and Inspection

## How long do refunds take?
Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after the returned item passes inspection at our Bhiwandi warehouse. The clock starts when inspection passes, not when you ship the item or request the return.

## What is inspection?
When your return reaches the Bhiwandi warehouse, our team checks that the item matches the return request: that it is the right product, is complete with accessories, and is in the condition you described (unopened, or opened and not damaged).

## How much will I get back?
The final refund amount is only confirmed after inspection. Any estimate shown earlier is not final. If the 10% restocking fee applies (opened BP-200, OX-02 or SC-03 returned within 15 days), it is deducted from the refund.

## Where does the money go?
Always back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card, account or UPI id.

## Can the assistant issue a refund?
No. The assistant cannot issue or approve refunds. Refunds are processed after inspection, and only a human support agent can approve exceptions.

## Subscription refunds
Care+ is not refundable and has no pro-rated refunds.

## My refund has not arrived
If more than 7 business days have passed since your return passed inspection, contact support with your order id.
