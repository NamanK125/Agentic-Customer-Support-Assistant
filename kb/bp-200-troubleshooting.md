# BP-200 Error Codes E1-E5

## E1: cuff not wrapped properly or air leak
The cuff is not wrapped correctly or air is leaking. Re-wrap the cuff snugly on your bare upper arm, with the tube running down the inside of the arm, and check the tube is firmly plugged in. If E1 repeats, the cuff may be damaged.

## E2: movement or talking detected
You moved or talked during the measurement. Sit with your back supported, feet flat, arm resting at heart level on a table, and stay still and quiet until the reading finishes. Then try again.

## E3: cuff overinflated
The cuff inflated too far. Release the cuff, wait a minute, make sure the cuff is the right size (22-42 cm arm), and measure again. If E3 keeps appearing, contact support.

## E4: battery low
The batteries are low. Replace all 4 × AA batteries with new ones. Low batteries are also a common cause of unreliable readings.

## E5: Bluetooth pairing failed
The BP-200 could not pair with the app. See the Bluetooth pairing guide: turn on phone Bluetooth, keep the phone close, and retry pairing from the app. E5 does not affect the measurement itself.

## Still not fixed?
If an error persists after you follow the steps, contact support with the error code. If the device is within warranty (2 years) it can be replaced.
