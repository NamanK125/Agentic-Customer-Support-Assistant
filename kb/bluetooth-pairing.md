# Bluetooth Pairing Troubleshooting

This guide applies to all PulseNest devices (BP-200, OX-02, TH-01, SC-03) and the Care+ app.

## Requirements
- Android 9 or newer, or iOS 15 or newer.
- Bluetooth switched on in your phone. On Android also allow the app the Nearby devices (or Location) permission.

## Steps
1. Open the PulseNest app and tap Add device.
2. Wake the device. On the BP-200 hold the Bluetooth button until the Bluetooth icon blinks.
3. Keep the phone within 1 metre of the device.
4. Choose your device in the app list and confirm.

## BP-200 shows E5
E5 means pairing failed. Switch Bluetooth off and on, close and reopen the app, remove the device from the phone's Bluetooth list (forget device) and try pairing again. Make sure the batteries are fresh; low batteries cause pairing to fail.

## Still not connecting?
- Pair only one device at a time.
- Restart the phone.
- Update the app and your phone's operating system.
- Do not pair from the phone's system Bluetooth settings; always pair from inside the app.

## Readings do not sync
Check that Care+ is active and that the app has the Bluetooth permission. Readings stored on the device are not lost; the BP-200 keeps 120 readings per user.
