# SC-03 Scale Safety - Pacemakers and Implanted Devices

## Pacemaker warning
Do not use the SC-03 body-composition feature if you have a pacemaker or any other implanted electronic medical device. The scale sends a very small electrical current through your feet (bioelectrical impedance, BIA). The body-composition figures are estimates and are not suitable for people with implants.

If you have an implanted device and still want to use the scale for weight only, ask your doctor first. PulseNest support cannot give medical advice on this.

## Troubleshooting the scale
- Place the scale on a hard, flat floor. Carpet gives wrong readings.
- Stand barefoot with dry feet, with both feet on the electrodes, to get body composition values.
- The maximum load is 180 kg. Above that the scale shows an overload message.
- If the scale will not turn on or shows a low battery symbol, replace the 4 × AAA batteries.
- Body-composition numbers vary through the day with water intake, so weigh at the same time each day.
