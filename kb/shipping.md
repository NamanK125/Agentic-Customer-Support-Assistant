# Shipping and Delivery

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping is free on orders of ₹999 or more. For orders below ₹999 the standard shipping fee is ₹79.

Standard delivery takes 3-6 business days in metro cities and 5-9 business days to other locations.

## Express shipping
Express shipping costs ₹149 and delivers in 1-2 business days. It is available only in Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune and Ahmedabad. Express is not available elsewhere.

Express shipping is charged even on orders above ₹999; the free-shipping offer applies to standard shipping only.

## Comparing the two
| | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free from ₹999, else ₹79 | ₹149 |
| Metro time | 3-6 business days | 1-2 business days |
| Non-metro time | 5-9 business days | Not available |

## When will my order arrive?
The delivery times above are estimates. A delivery date for your specific order is only confirmed after your order has shipped and the courier issues an ETA. Before that, we cannot promise an exact date. Track your order in the app or ask the assistant with your order id (looks like PN-10xxx).
