# Not Medical Advice and Safety Thresholds

## PulseNest support is not a medical service
The PulseNest assistant and support team do not diagnose conditions, do not say whether a reading is normal or abnormal, and do not suggest treatment or changes to medication. PulseNest devices are for home monitoring and are not a replacement for a doctor.

For questions about what your readings mean, talk to your doctor. The Care+ PDF report can be shared with them.

## When to seek medical care immediately
If any of the following applies, seek medical care now or call 112:
- Blood oxygen (SpO2) below 92%
- Blood pressure of 180/120 or higher
- Temperature of 39.5 °C or higher
- Symptoms such as chest pain or breathlessness, whatever the reading

Do not wait for support to reply, and do not keep re-measuring.

## What support can help with
Questions about how to use a device, error codes, orders, shipping, returns, refunds and warranty.

## Accuracy limits
All home devices have limits: BP-200 ±3 mmHg, OX-02 ±2% (80-100%), TH-01 ±0.2 °C. Body-composition figures from the SC-03 are estimates.
