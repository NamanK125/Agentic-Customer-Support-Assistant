# PulseNest Scale S3 (SC-03) - Product FAQ

## What is the SC-03?
The PulseNest Scale S3 is a smart body-composition scale. It costs ₹2,999 and has a 1 year warranty. It syncs to the Care+ app over Bluetooth.

## What is the maximum weight?
The scale supports users up to 180 kg.

## How does body composition work?
The scale uses bioelectrical impedance (BIA): a very small electrical signal passes through your feet. Body fat, muscle and similar figures are estimates, not clinical measurements. Weight itself is a direct measurement.

## Who should not use the body-composition feature?
People with a pacemaker or any other implanted medical device should not use the SC-03. Please also see the scale safety article. If you are unsure, ask your doctor first.

## What batteries does it use?
4 × AAA batteries.

## Can I return it after opening?
An opened SC-03 that is not defective can be returned within 15 days of delivery with a 10% restocking fee. Unopened, it can be returned within 30 days for a full refund.
