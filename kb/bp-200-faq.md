# PulseNest Cuff BP-200 - Product FAQ

## What is the BP-200?
The PulseNest Cuff BP-200 is an upper-arm blood pressure monitor with Bluetooth. It costs ₹3,499 and comes with a 2 year warranty, the longest of any PulseNest device.

## Which arm sizes does it fit?
The cuff fits upper-arm circumferences of 22-42 cm. If your arm is outside this range, the cuff will not give reliable readings and you should not use it. Measure around the middle of your upper arm with a soft tape.

## How accurate is it?
Pressure accuracy is ±3 mmHg.

## How many readings can it store?
The BP-200 stores 120 readings for each of 2 users, so 240 readings in total. Switch between User 1 and User 2 with the user button before measuring.

## What batteries does it use?
It uses 4 × AA batteries, which last for roughly 300 readings. When the batteries run low the display shows error E4. Replace all four batteries together.

## Does it work with the app?
Yes. The BP-200 pairs over Bluetooth with the PulseNest Care+ app (Android 9+ or iOS 15+). Readings sync to the app so you can share PDF reports with your doctor. See the Bluetooth pairing guide if pairing fails (error E5).

## Does the BP-200 tell me if my reading is normal?
No. PulseNest support does not interpret readings. Please discuss your readings with a doctor. See the "Not medical advice" article.
