# Oximeter Poor or Unstable Readings

If your OX-02 shows no reading, a flickering number or a "poor signal" result, try these steps.

## Warm your hands
Cold fingers reduce blood flow and cause poor readings. Warm your hands, rub them together, and rest them for a minute before measuring.

## Remove nail polish
Nail polish, especially dark colours, and artificial nails can block the light sensor. Remove the polish or try another finger without it.

## Stay still
Movement causes unstable readings. Rest your hand on a table or your lap, stay still and do not talk while measuring. Wait for the number to settle for a few seconds before you note it.

## Fit and batteries
Insert the finger fully, with the nail side facing up. Check the 2 × AAA batteries: weak batteries can cause the device to switch off or read erratically.

## Good to know
The device is accurate to ±2% in the 80-100% range and measures from 70-100%.

## Safety
If you see a reading below 92% or you feel breathless or have chest pain, do not troubleshoot. Seek medical care now or call 112. See the safety article.

## Still a problem?
If the OX-02 still gives no reading after these steps, contact support. It is covered by a 1 year warranty.
